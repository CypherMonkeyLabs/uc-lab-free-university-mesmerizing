## What changed in v21

The campus keeps the work a voice, UC, or contact-center engineer does now: CUCM, CUBE, SIP, Webex Calling and Contact Center, Teams Phone, UCCE, PCCE, CUIC, CCaaS platforms, E911, QoS, and troubleshooting.

WebRTC remains, with one reason: browser agent desktops. Linux remains as the CUCM command line, not as a general Linux course.

Removed from this edition: the trading-turret track, the Asterisk course, the generic machine-learning chapter, career-ladder pages, and the repeated practicum templates.
