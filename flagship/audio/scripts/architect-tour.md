# How to view the lab

Two voices. Maya asks. Jules answers. Generated offline with espeak-ng.

**Maya:** I just opened the UC Lab. What am I looking at?

**Jules:** The first screen is the call path. A phone, CUCM, CUBE, and the cloud, orbiting as one picture.

**Maya:** Where do I go if I want the drawings, not the essay?

**Jules:** Open Diagrams or Flows. Click a drawing and it opens large. Then play, pause, or step one hop at a time.

**Maya:** And if I want the counts?

**Jules:** Open Charts. Every bar names where the number came from. If a figure is only a teaching example, the page says example.

**Maya:** What are the themes for?

**Jules:** Neon, holographic, cyberpunk, sunset, and light. They recolor the scene. There is also a light and dark switch, and a language menu.
