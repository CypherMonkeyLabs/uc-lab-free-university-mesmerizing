Firewall cheat sheet

  • Phone to CUCM: 69/UDP, 6970/TCP, 5060, 5061, 2000 or 2443, 3804
  • Gateway to CUCM: 2427/UDP or 1720/TCP, plus RTP
  • RTP between media hosts: UDP 16384–32767 unless you have changed it
  • Apps to CUCM: 2748/2749 CTI, 8443 AXL
  • CUCM to the world: 123 NTP, 389/636/3268/3269 LDAP, 443 CSSM, 22 backup
  • Keep ephemeral 32768–61000 open on the node or phones drop

Source: CUCM 15 TCP and UDP port usage.
