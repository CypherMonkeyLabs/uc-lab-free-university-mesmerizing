Troubleshooting cases

29 branching cases. Each one starts from a symptom. Wrong branches say why they are wrong and send you back.

  1. A PSTN call connects. The caller hears the carrier. The carrier hears nothing.
  2. A lobby phone dials a national number and gets reorder immediately.
  3. A laptop off the VPN cannot register. Desk phones in the office are fine.
  4. Monday morning, nobody outside can sign in. A few internal services warn about tomcat.
  5. A branch user dials 911. The call connects. The PSAP calls back and reaches a headquarters receptionist.
  6. A forwarded call reaches Unity. Every caller hears the company greeting.
  7. Users dial a national number and get reorder. Four-digit calls still work.
  8. A translation is supposed to turn 9.1 plus ten digits into +E.164. Callers hear dial tone again, then reorder.
  9. The caller sits in queue, then the script selects an agent. The desktop shows Reserved. The phone does not ring.
  10. Two outbound dial peers share a destination pattern. About half the calls use the expensive peer.
  11. The WAN is back. Branch phones still show registered on the router.
  12. A Jabber client outside connects. Neither side hears anything.
  13. A user in a two-cluster company signs into the cluster that does not own the directory number.
  14. The user is signed into Jabber. The desk phone is unregistered in RIS.
  15. WAN down. Branch phones are on the router. Webex Calling users in the same company do not ring.
  16. A lobby phone reaches the CEO's directory number. It should not.
  17. After a class-of-service cleanup, the lobby cannot dial emergency. Executives still can.
  18. A national call fails. The CUBE debug stays empty.
  19. The same phone dials out. Inbound to its directory number from the carrier fails.
  20. A PSTN call that used to connect now fails after the SIP profile was set to Mandatory.
  21. collab-edge is correct. Outside Jabber still will not start MRA. Desk phones are fine.
  22. Jabber on the iPhone works while the app is open. After the screen locks, calls vanish. Desktop Jabber is fine.
  23. DNS is published. A new X14.3 pair has no MRA registrations.
  24. The lobby cannot dial the international route pattern. A four-digit code on the same phone can.
  25. A phone that just appeared in the cluster can dial national numbers. Nobody built a line for it.
  26. The pilot matches. Three phones ring. The fourth, which is first in the line group, stays dark.
  27. A user dials the pilot from their own phone and hears the opening greeting.
  28. DNA shows one calling-party mask. The SIP trunk trace selected another.
  29. Every hunt member pressed HLog. New callers still sit in queue and hear music.
