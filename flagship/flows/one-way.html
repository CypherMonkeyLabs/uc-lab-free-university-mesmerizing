SDP c= and RTP

One-way audio

The ladder can be perfect while one RTP direction never arrives.

Open both legs before you restart CallManager. A CSS miss does not connect a call and then drop one direction. QoS can still hurt a call that has two RTP streams. It does not explain a missing answer.
