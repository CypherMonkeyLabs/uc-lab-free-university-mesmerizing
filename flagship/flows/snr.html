Single Number Reach

SNR extends one enterprise number to a remote destination. The remote leg is another call, with its own timing.

If the mobile rings and audio fails, treat the remote leg as a PSTN call: gateway, codec, and calling-number presentation. The desk phone's CSS is not the mobile's path.
