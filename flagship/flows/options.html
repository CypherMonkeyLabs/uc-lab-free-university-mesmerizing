RFC 3261

OPTIONS keepalive

OPTIONS asks a target if it is alive. It is not a call, and a timeout can take the target out of service.

If half your calls use the expensive carrier, check preference and whether keepalive has busied out the cheap one. Do not 'fix' it by adding a second identical destination-pattern at the same preference.
