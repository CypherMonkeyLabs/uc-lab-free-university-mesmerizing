🇺🇸🇪🇺 Regions engineers actually name

Only the rules below are cited. A country list for a calling plan changes. This page does not freeze one. Anything marked example is a teaching label, not coverage.

🇺🇸 United States

The FCC’s MLTS rules, from Kari’s Law and RAY BAUM’s Act section 506, require multi-line telephone systems to let a user dial 911 directly, to notify someone on site, and to send a dispatchable location. Read the current FCC page before you design a prefix or a location source.

FCC: Multi-line telephone systems

🇪🇺 European Union

Regulation (EU) 2016/679 is the GDPR. A recording of a person’s voice can be personal data. This lab does not invent a retention period or a member-state recording statute. Read the regulation and the recorder vendor’s current guide.

EUR-Lex: Regulation (EU) 2016/679

Calling-plan countries

Microsoft publishes which countries and regions have Calling Plans and Audio Conferencing. That list changes. Direct Routing is a customer SBC and is a different model. Cisco publishes Webex Calling availability in Webex Help. This lab links both and copies neither list.

Example numbering labels

Example, not a coverage claim. ITU-T E.164 is the international public numbering plan.
