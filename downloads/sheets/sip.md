# SIP in one page

- A transaction is not a dialog.
- 180 is alerting. 200 accepts. ACK finishes a 2xx.
- REGISTER stores a Contact.
- Early offer puts SDP in the INVITE.
- PRACK answers a reliable 183.
- RTP may skip the proxy.
- Source: RFC 3261, RFC 3264, RFC 3262.
