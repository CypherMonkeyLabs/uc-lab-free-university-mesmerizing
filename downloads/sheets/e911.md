# Emergency calling note

- Study notes, not legal advice and not an emergency plan.
- The FCC MLTS page describes direct 911 dialing, notification, and dispatchable location for covered US systems.
- Cisco Emergency Responder maps a switch port to an ERL and an ELIN. Confirm Release 15 before you build.
- A PSAP callback is a separate call toward that ELIN.
- Rules and carriers outside the pages this lab opened stay on a confirm row.
